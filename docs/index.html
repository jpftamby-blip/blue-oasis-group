Incorporated, operated, defunct.

Blue Oasis Group Ltd (originally registered as Blue Oasis Properties Ltd) was incorporated in Mauritius on 15 October 2010. From that date it operated as a real estate and construction services agency under the leadership of Jean-Paul Tamby as Managing Director. The company ceased operating around April 2015 and is now recorded as defunct on the Mauritius Registrar of Companies. It has not traded since.

Scope of work

What the agency did.

Blue Oasis Group offered real estate and construction services across four areas, serving both commercial and individual clients in Mauritius.

  • Commercial projects Offices, resorts, hotels, retail and mall developments.
  • Construction advisory services Consultancy and technical advice on construction and development projects.
  • Individual projects Homes, villas and bungalows for private clients.
  • Agency services Full real estate brokerage, valuation and after-sales support.
